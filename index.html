01 THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROBLEMS

Hi, I'm Aaron.

I'm a student drawn to pure and applied mathematics—and to what happens when a good idea becomes something people can play, use, or explore.

My work moves between olympiad problem solving, mathematical modeling, and game development. I've trained at Canada's IMO team summer camp, contributed to Mini Airways, and helped bring online games to more players through translation.

Outside that world, you'll find me at a piano, behind a camera, or underwater. Different ways of paying attention.
